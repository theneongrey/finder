Two small pill primitives. `Badge` is a filled tint pill for counts ("5 Umfragen"), the "Ohne Projekt" tag, and role labels. `StatusDot` is the dot+label pattern for open/closed poll state.

```jsx
<Badge variant="accent">5 Umfragen</Badge>
<Badge variant="manager">Verwalter</Badge>
<StatusDot label="Läuft" tone="positive" />
```
