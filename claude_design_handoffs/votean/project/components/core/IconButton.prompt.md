Circular icon-only button. `surface` (white + soft shadow) is the floating back button on scrolled headers; `ghost` is the muted kebab/more trigger that darkens slightly on hover; `dark` is the sand-filled close button used on the share sheet.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="chevron-left" variant="surface" onClick={goBack} />
<IconButton icon="kebab" variant="ghost" size={28} />
```
